# Country identities and marketing entry

Design plan, reviewed before implementation (2026-10-16).

Shared tokens: canopy #163e35, paper #fbfcf8, mist #edf3f1, pollen #e9c86b, river #216779. Country accents remain in this natural family: Argentina river blue, Brazil palm green, Costa Rica deep forest, Portugal Atlantic blue. Georgia is the travel-journal display; Arial is the interface face. Text remains left aligned except physical passport objects and seals.

Layout:
```
Home:      [CityPassport story + actions | illustrated travel journal]
           [discover / collect / keep] [Iguazú example] [choose a country]
Countries: [introduction]
           [Argentina postcard] [Brazil postcard]
           [Costa Rica postcard] [Portugal postcard]
Country:   [country title + colors | country illustration]
           [available registry cities] [passport call to action]
```

Review: the country directory becomes an actual selection step, not another general landing page. The marketing page uses the original landscape and physical passport as its memorable object, rather than generic product screenshots, commercial promises or activity counters. Source listings remain references; sample stamps remain samples. Country selection is a browser preference, never identity or authorization. The neutral brand introduction remains stable regardless of that preference. Direct city URLs theme the shell by their registry country, independently of stored preferences.
