# Passport and collectible motion

The original illustrated waterfall stays on the discovery page. The miniature Three.js waterfall now belongs to a sample collectible: open the passport, press a sample stamp, then select it to reveal its landscape and story. Motion responds to those actions.

Keep the journal palette: canopy #163e35, river #216779, mist #edf3f1, paper #fbfcf8, pollen #e9c86b, ink #173b34. Georgia carries the travel-journal headlines, system sans the interface. The small perforated waterfall stamp is the focal point; its detail pairs the miniature on the left with readable copy on the right, stacking on mobile.

Sample stamps never alter the real collection. Existing collected stamps open their recorded name and date; no waterfall association is invented for unrelated places. Each future stamp can receive its own artwork when its content is available.

The native modal provides focus containment, Escape dismissal and focus restoration. The landscape mounts only while open and disposes on dismissal. Reduced motion retains the SVG with explicit animation opt-in; WebGL failure retains the illustration and information. Rendering pauses offscreen, in hidden tabs and on manual pause. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5 and frames at 30fps. The stamp press dynamically loads Three.js on click and disposes after the action.

References: https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer.html and https://threejs.org/manual/pages/how-to-dispose-of-objects.html
